## 4. Photos

- Photos are re-encoded on your phone before upload; this is how location and EXIF data are removed. Information visible in the picture itself (text, addresses, people) is not removed, so check before sharing.
- Before publishing, photos are checked automatically with **Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch** (EU endpoint). Unacceptable photos are not published; borderline ones are published blurred with a "sensitive" label.
- For Rate questions, your phone looks for human faces on the device itself (Apple Vision) and blocks photos with faces. No face template (biometric data) is created or sent to our servers. If Apple's sensitive content analysis is turned on on your phone, it also runs on the device.
- Published question photos are stored at addresses **anyone with the link can open**, so they can appear on share pages and in notifications. These addresses cannot be guessed, but if the address of a private account's photo is shared with someone, that person can open it.

## 5. Special categories of data

We don't ask for sensitive data such as health, religion, political opinions or biometric data. If you choose to share such information in a question, comment or photo, it is processed only as you made it public and only to publish your content (GDPR Art. 9(2)(e); KVKK Art. 6/3), and not for any other purpose. Sharing such information about others is against our Terms of Use.

## 6. Who can see what

- **Everyone:** username, avatar, follower/following counts, and for public accounts their questions, comments, Resences, follow lists and question totals (percentages). Public accounts' questions can also be viewed on the web through a shared link without signing in; results are not shown there.
- **Only approved followers:** a private account's questions, Resences and follow lists.
- **No other user:** your votes, age range, gender, star sign, interests, the people you block and your reports.
- People who see public content can save it on their own devices; deleting it from Sence does not delete those copies.

## 7. Who we share data with

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. To run Sence we use:

- **Google Firebase / Google Cloud** (database, file storage, server functions, authentication, Cloud Messaging) and **Google Cloud Vision** (photo checks): as processors acting on our behalf and instructions.
- **Apple** (notification delivery via APNs; Sign in with Apple) and **Google** (Google Sign-In): as independent controllers under their own privacy policies for the account you link.
- **Competent authorities:** when required by law or a binding order, and only to the extent required.
- Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages; no user data is stored there. The shared question page fetches the question from our Firebase servers.

## 10. When you delete your account

You can delete it in **Settings → Account → Delete account**, in two steps; **deletion is permanent.** Your profile, private profile data, questions and photos, comments, Resences, follows and requests, blocks, reports, vote records and notifications are deleted right away, and your Apple/Google link is revoked (your actual Apple or Google account is not deleted). Deletion is completed in our service providers' systems within 30 days.

Not deleted: anonymous vote totals on others' questions (so their results don't change), activity logs for the legal period, your username as it appears in notifications already delivered to others, and copies others saved on their own devices. Your username is not given to anyone else for 30 days, to prevent impersonation.

## 11. Automated processing

The Trending feed is ranked automatically using your interests and the state of each question; it only affects what you see first and has no legal or similarly significant effect on you. Question and comment text passes an automatic profanity filter, and photos pass automatic image checks. If content was not published because of an automatic check, you can ask for human review by emailing us.

## 12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). On the server we use rules that allow only authorised access, app verification with App Check and rate limits; activity logs are kept apart from app data. Only authorised people who handle moderation can access reports and moderation records. No system is completely secure; if a data breach occurs, we will notify the authorities and you as required by law.

## 13. Age limit

Sence is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from people under 18. If you notice that someone under 18 has an account, email us; we will close it and delete its data except what we must keep by law.

## 14. Your rights

**Under the GDPR** you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can also complain to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live or work (list on the European Data Protection Board website, edpb.europa.eu).

**Under KVKK (Türkiye)** you have the rights listed in Art. 11, including to learn whether your data is processed and request information, to learn the purpose and the recipients in Türkiye and abroad, to request correction or erasure and notification of these to recipients, to object to an adverse result produced solely by automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. If we reject your request or don't reply in time, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.
