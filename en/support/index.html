Common questions

I got a new phone. How do I get my account back?

If you linked your account to Apple or Google, tap “I already have an account” on the welcome screen and sign in with the same one. Without a link the old account can't be recovered, so we recommend linking it in Settings → Account.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm in two steps. Your nickname, questions, comments and follows are deleted for good; your votes stay in the counts, without your identity. Your nickname becomes available to others after 30 days.

How do I report a question or a person?

Open the “…” menu at the top right of the card and choose Report question. To report or block a person, use the “…” menu on their profile. You can also press and hold a comment to report it.

How do I unblock someone?

Go to Settings → Blocked accounts and tap Unblock. The person isn't notified when you block them.

Can I change my nickname?

Yes: Settings → My info → Nickname. You can change it once every 30 days.

I'm not getting notifications.

Make sure notifications are on in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Sence. In the app, choose which ones you want under Settings → Notifications.

Legal requests and content removal requests can go to the same address. Please include a link to the question or profile in question.
