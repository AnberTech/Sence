How it works

A minute to ask, seconds to get answers.

  1. 01

    Ask

    Add a photo and write your question in up to 99 characters. Pick a category and a time limit: 10 minutes, 1 to 24 hours, or no limit.

Ask freely

Nobody on Sence is there to judge you. They're just answering your question.

  • Nickname only

    No real name, phone number or password. Pick a nickname and you're in.

  • Private votes

    Nobody is shown who voted for what. Even the person asking only sees numbers.

  • Checked photos

    Every photo is checked before it goes live. Location data is removed while it's still on your phone.

  • Report and block

    Report a question that bothers you and block someone with one tap. You can also make your account private.

  • 18+ only

    Sence is a community for adults. Rude language is caught before it's posted.

  • Your account, kept

    Link Apple or Google so a new phone doesn't mean a lost account. You can delete your account in the app.

Frequently asked questions

What is Sence?

An app where you ask about things you can't decide on, under a nickname, and the crowd votes. There are three kinds of questions: Yes/No, Pick (2–4 options) and Rate (0–10). “Sence” is Turkish for “what do you think?”.

Does it cost anything?

No. Sence is free to download and use.

Can anyone see who voted for what?

No. Voters are never shown anywhere. Everyone, including the person asking, sees only percentages and the number of votes.

Why are there no passwords?

Your account is created on your phone; you just pick a nickname. So you don't lose it when you switch phones, link it to Apple or Google in Settings, then use “I already have an account” on the new phone.

How long does my question stay open?

You choose: 10 minutes, 1, 6, 12 or 24 hours, or no limit at all. When time is up the result locks and you get a notification.

Which devices does it run on?

Sence is coming very soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad (iOS 17.6 or later) and to Google Play for Android.

Which languages are supported?

English and Turkish for now. More languages are on the way.
